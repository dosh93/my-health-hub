A personal project bringing Garmin and Oura workouts, sleep and wellness records together, with a clear label for every source.

Both sources stay visible

The planned iPhone app will display records from both services separately. It will preserve the original source and available sleep scores, rather than replacing them with a new combined score.

Starting with a local prototype

Development currently focuses on downloading the owner's own data to their Mac. Oura integration is being configured. No public sign-up or hosted health-data service is available.

This project is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Garmin or Oura.
